4. Use the measured phase gains and costs to refine the Ni choice. Recheck J and phase stability at the exact final compositions; do not assume Ni independence. If no qualified point exists yet, return to a bounded refinement rather than rename a violation as success.
5. Record one preferred composition and a justified alternative, with a requirement-by-requirement rationale and the unfavorable comparisons. Request fresh verification of the preferred candidate and reference, then a scientific review. A verified repeat confirms calculation consistency; it does not settle the tradeoff judgment.
