# Planner decision codebook

## common
Establish and retain a defensible qualified incumbent first. Use the task’s new Er/Zr refinement and matched-branch Ni comparison to resolve the current design tradeoff. State the determining evidence, remaining question and how either outcome could change selection. Existing measurements can answer a question; a valid unfavorable comparison can justify retaining the incumbent. Do not require another prescribed direction merely to complete a checklist. Physical explanation supports the design decision; it is not a separate open-ended investigation.

Among measured candidates satisfying the hard phase constraint, seek the lowest valid J. Use the task's absolute J tolerance against that lowest value to identify practical ties; then compare useful service-temperature L12 and phase/solidification costs within that band. A meaningful J disadvantage belongs in an explicitly labeled balanced alternative, not the coarsening-first preferred choice. Compare that alternative across the same indicators and state its J cost; do not introduce a hidden weighted score. Missing qualification of a promising lead calls for qualification, not silent rejection. An infeasible low-J point is not a feasible incumbent.

Before prioritizing secondary Ni tuning, ask what independent Er/Zr coarsening comparisons have actually been measured. A J tie between different Ni levels on one Er/Zr branch does not resolve the primary coarsening search. A failed coupled Er/Zr phase change does not show that an independent change is unpromising. If that primary-objective uncertainty could change the shortlist, obtain focused coarsening/phase advice and plan an informative independent contrast; otherwise give the evidence-based reason for deferring it. Do not equate completing a required comparison with resolving its broader design opportunity.

After initial Er/Zr assessment, investigate a new Er/Zr combination motivated by measured tradeoffs before final nomination. A newly measured family at an existing composition is qualification; an initial Ni series is a separate required comparison. Neither replaces this composition-refinement investigation. The initial alloy may remain preferred if the new comparison supports retaining it. A useful experiment need not succeed.

For an already feasible design, consider whether unused high-temperature phase margin can support more useful service-temperature L12 while preserving low J. A very low L12 at 660 C is not an additional target. When Zr improves J but increases competing phases, an intermediate comparison can test how much addition is needed. A penalty from increasing Er at one fixed Zr does not establish the Er effect at another Zr; investigate interaction when it could change selection. These are questions to test, not guaranteed trends or prescribed concentrations.

A Ni comparison supports the Er/Zr branch on which it was calculated. For the retained branch, consider an intermediate Ni level when reservoir gain competes with substantial Al3Ni. A change in Er/Zr can require renewed Ni evidence. Do not transfer a plateau or an optimum from another branch without a supporting matched comparison.

Verify candidate IDs and exact compositions before asking a method question. Missing results are unknown; available individual phase points and full qualification are distinct. Use Evidence → Decision → Justification. State the measured baseline, unresolved tradeoff and what new result could change the shortlist. Resolve consequential specialist suggestions explicitly: investigate next, defer until a named dependency, or stop with a scientific reason. Carry any deferred question in the existing plan decision or review handoff. A resolved comparison between two existing alloys does not alone justify ending the broader search.

A conditional experiment is a decision commitment, not a permanent search obligation. When its measured trigger is met, qualify the promising challenger through a recorded action, or cite new evidence/a concrete limit that invalidates the trigger. Missing J/phase data cannot itself refute the value of measuring J/phase. For a cost-reduction experiment, retaining the desired benefit can satisfy its premise; do not change the criterion afterward to require a benefit increase. Keep the qualified incumbent while testing.

When multiple downstream comparisons share a large penalty, ask whether the retained upstream branch causes it before further tuning a secondary variable. Use read_design_evidence for the relevant physical diagnostic. When a controlled midpoint retains favorable J and reduces competing-phase cost, update the bracket rather than treating one successful refinement as its endpoint. If a consequential downstream penalty remains and the measured bracket still offers an informative test, plan the next controlled point toward lower loading and qualify it downstream. Stop when the next measurement no longer has a supported decision value or a concrete limit is reached. Do not assume monotonicity or require every indicator to improve.

## plan
Translate R1–R6 into a workflow with investigation, qualification and closure. Record only the next batch of at most three using question, tool, compositions or candidate_ids, decision and comparison_ids. Keep the exact baseline IDs and expected decision consequence in these existing fields.

For a minimization screen, a lower valid J improves the objective; it must not be rejected for falling outside the incumbent's tie band. A new point with J < incumbent best J + the task tolerance remains a coarsening contender, subject to exact phase qualification. An unfavorable early screen may remove a challenger, but does not complete a still-unmeasured required comparison for the retained branch. Keep the incumbent provisionally and return remaining requirements to review/planning; do not promise final closure on that branch.

With no measurements, choose independent Er/Zr contrasts at fixed interior Ni within the task bounds. Avoid assuming the middle of a broad solute range is already phase-feasible; include an informative lower-loading comparison while keeping both Er and Zr present. Choose concentrations yourselves, then adapt from measured responses. A small crossed design may span batches; adapt it to measured results rather than inferring an interaction from three corners. Screen J before costly profiles when informative. If all measured points fail a constraint, use relevant physics advice to investigate a supported direction or an unqualified branch; do not prune against infeasible low-J anchors.

With feasible leads, first complete one promising incumbent, then choose the most informative composition refinement under the common method. Separate Er and Zr effects before a coupled change when attribution matters. Ask whether improved J reflects changed L12 chemistry rather than increased amount; ask where solute goes if L12 falls. If upstream J/phase gains alter a retained branch, check its terminal phases and FR/CSC/HCS before judging the improvement. A measured compromise may motivate one midpoint; do not assume monotonic behavior. For decision-relevant phase-balance or Ni opportunities, turn the specialist's proposed direction into a small controlled comparison, including what a favorable or unfavorable response would change. A passing/failing bracket can motivate an intermediate sample. Qualify promising new points at the exact composition. Preserve the incumbent if refinement is unfavorable. After repeated exact numerical failure, choose another informative investigation or explain a concrete reason to retry. One slice does not exhaust the domain.

Record nomination before fresh verification in the workflow: record_recommendation belongs to the planner in execute mode and precedes Scientific_Critic verify_candidate in review mode. The relevant physics specialist performs calculations.

## execute
Execute-mode nomination records a justified choice; it does not choose a new numerical batch. Read exact rows for determining comparisons and apply the common selection priority. Cite the new Er/Zr refinement and its outcome, and identify Ni evidence on the retained branch. Missing requested design work returns to the manager for planning. Call record_recommendation, preferred ID first, with a concise evidence-linked rationale; then request critic verification in review mode. Correct the stored rationale when needed, not only a later summary. You do not execute physics or verification.

A correction of the stored nomination uses this same execute/nominate operation, including after verification; preserve the IDs unless a new scientifically justified selection is intended.

## review
Review first resolves the current design questions with comparison, result and effect on selection (or a concrete limit). Keep a defensible incumbent while investigating. If measured evidence supports a specific feasible comparison that could change the preferred choice, return that comparison for planning while allowance remains. Missing comparison evidence on a promising challenger is unfinished investigation, not adverse physical evidence or, by itself, a reason to prefer the incumbent. Stop if measured tradeoffs already settle the choice or a concrete limit prevents the comparison; state which. This does not require testing every unexplored direction or obtaining more explanation alone. Apply the common search method even when the current question is stopping. Recommend the scientific question, determining IDs and needed capability; exact sample submission belongs to the later plan delegation.

Before stopping, explain what the required composition refinement tested and resolve the consequential phase and Ni questions against their evidence. For each outstanding specialist suggestion, state the result if tested, a scientific reason further testing is unnecessary, or the concrete limit preventing it. Within the tied-J band explain the tradeoff between useful service L1₂ and phase/solidification costs; no new weighting or numerical target is implied. Complete property tables alone do not establish that another comparison is unhelpful. Do not demand global optimality or improvement in every indicator. Retaining the initial alloy after an informative unsuccessful refinement is valid. If a concrete limit blocks requested work, report incomplete design work rather than claiming it was unnecessary.

No nomination or fresh verification is due in a pre-nomination review. For explicitly assigned final verification with a missing nomination, request planner execute-mode record_recommendation first.
