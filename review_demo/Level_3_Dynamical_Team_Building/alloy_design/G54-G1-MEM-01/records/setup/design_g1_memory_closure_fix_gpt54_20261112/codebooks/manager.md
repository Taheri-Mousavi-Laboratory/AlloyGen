# Coordination codebook

1. For an empty investigation, ask for a requirements-to-workflow map covering the assigned scientific investigations and an informative first experiment. Keep task order clear: initial Er/Zr exploration precedes the later controlled Ni/Scheil comparison; do not promote a later comparison requirement into an initial-batch requirement. For a saved investigation, inspect both measured coverage and outstanding scientific work. A complete property table does not imply that requested design refinement occurred. Subsequent assignments address the most consequential unresolved question from measured results, within the task's priorities.
2. After a team returns, distinguish correct scoped work from completion of the design mission. Record the answer, remaining question and next deliverable in working memory, reconcile existing note IDs, then delegate the needed work. Reserve a standalone final report for whole-mission closure or a justified incomplete stop. For a valid unfavorable result, ask for a design/refinement decision. For an incorrect claim, request correction against its exact records. For missing qualification of a promising composition, assign the missing calculation. A valid negative result is useful evidence, not an incorrect report.
3. Match advising expertise to the current unresolved quantity: phase expertise for phase-constraint recovery, coarsening expertise for J, and solidification expertise for Scheil tradeoffs. For planning request the planner, relevant physics expertise and critic. For a narrow numerical execution ask the builder for its calculation owner and critic, and reuse that focused team for later work of the same kind. Reusing a broad team retains all its members' turns; asking them to stay brief does not remove those turns. Use broad collaboration when the scientific decision needs it. A group able to record a plan is not necessarily qualified to advise on its physics. A reused name loads its saved roster, not the full previous conversation. Pass determining findings and evidence IDs explicitly. The builder selects members; membership changes are not a success target.
4. If a measured conditional trigger already establishes a useful next qualification, carry its evidence and decision into plan mode directly; do not reopen it as a generic question of whether the incumbent is defensible. Select expertise for the unresolved property (including coarsening for J), then execute the recorded action. A changed decision needs new evidence or a concrete limit. Use a focused design review when the next scientific decision remains unresolved. When a promising lead already needs a specified missing calculation family, carry that accepted qualification question directly into plan mode, then execute the recorded action; an extra stopping review is unnecessary. Carry outstanding specialist suggestions and determining evidence IDs into the assignment: can another small experiment change the preference, or is stopping justified? Coverage alone is not the reason to stop. Prioritize a fully qualified incumbent, then the most consequential Er/Zr and Ni questions; do not turn every method suggestion into a mandatory extra test. Keep requested refinement distinct from missing qualification. After this review, act on its conclusion; do not repeat the checkpoint automatically. A proposed next experiment is not yet a recorded action: delegate plan mode before reporting that planning is complete. Before secondary Ni tuning, have relevant specialists check whether independent Er/Zr J evidence remains missing; a coupled phase failure or same-branch J tie does not resolve that search. If investigation is justified, delegate plan then execute. Otherwise delegate nomination in execute mode with execute_intent=nominate. Retain the feasible candidate while alternatives are investigated. A feasibility repair does not by itself establish the best feasible compromise; completing missing properties is qualification, not a new-composition refinement.
5. Scoped acceptance does not end the mission when requested work remains. An infeasible low-J lead is not a feasible incumbent or a rejection threshold for other branches. After an exact solver retry fails, ask what other informative investigation remains; one failed recipe does not exhaust the design domain. An incorrect claim goes back to its responsible specialist; a genuine design limitation returns to planning. Do not change reviewers to make an infeasible alloy acceptable, or demand a rejection/rebuild merely to create an extra round.
6. Before nomination, check that a new Er/Zr comparison was calculated after initial assessment and that final Ni support belongs to the retained branch. Do not relabel qualification or the initial Ni series as that refinement. Assign unresolved optimization questions rather than only choosing between two saved IDs; the planner chooses new samples. Carry consequential specialist suggestions into each assignment with their evidence IDs and unresolved question. Request an explicit disposition: investigate, defer with a dependency/limit, or resolve with a scientific reason. A correct result or complete table does not settle an untested improvement question. Before numerical execution, use execute_intent=calculate and check that current_action names the requested calculation and compositions and is ready; otherwise delegate planning first. A missing plan is not missing expertise and must not trigger a rebuild. Nomination is a separate execute-mode operation and does not authorize an unplanned calculation. Obtain the planner's justified choice through record_recommendation in execute mode with execute_intent=nominate, then assign Scientific_Critic to perform verify_candidate in review mode and final scientific review. If a prerequisite is missing, follow its next_delegation instead of repeating an incompatible review assignment. In the final comparison, include the reference, preferred candidate and any qualified alternative. Explain favorable, tied and unfavorable indicators relative to the reference as well as between candidates, using exact saved values and sources. Include unknowns and limitations. A positive primary objective does not erase secondary costs. Stop when the task and assigned investigations are supported, or report the specific unresolved question and concrete limit. A correct qualified incumbent supports retaining it during exploration, not treating an untested improvement question as resolved.

Selection policy shared with the specialists: Among measured candidates satisfying the hard phase constraint, seek the lowest valid J. Use the task's absolute J tolerance against that lowest value to identify practical ties; then compare useful service-temperature L12 and phase/solidification costs within that band. Report the high-temperature L12 constraint separately as pass/fail, outside favorable/unfavorable indicator comparisons. A meaningful J disadvantage belongs in an explicitly labeled tradeoff alternative, not the coarsening-first preferred choice. Missing qualification of a promising lead calls for qualification, not silent rejection. An infeasible low-J point is not a feasible incumbent.

## Assignment patterns

Adapt these to measured evidence and pass the determining IDs; exact samples remain the planner's decision.
- Establish an incumbent: "Identify the promising feasible lead and qualify its missing calculation families before opening more branches. State what is still unknown."
- Design refinement: "Use the measured Er/Zr contrast to identify the current J, service-phase or competing-phase tradeoff. Choose one controlled comparison whose favorable or unfavorable result could change the shortlist." Complete downstream Scheil evidence when that refined composition could be retained.
- Ni choice: "At the retained Er/Zr branch, compare useful ternary reservoir with competing phases and FR/CSC/HCS. Determine whether the measured comparison justifies a Ni choice or another level would materially affect it."
- Stopping review: "Do measured tradeoffs settle the preferred choice, or is there a specific feasible comparison that could change it? Check required evidence and the decision consequence. If such a comparison remains within allowance, return it for planning while retaining the incumbent; otherwise state the evidence or concrete limit supporting closure. Missing comparison support alone is not an adverse physical result."

If a returned review both identifies a feasible comparison that could change preference and recommends stopping solely because that evidence is missing, keep the question open in memory and delegate focused planning. A nomination eligibility requirement must not become a reason to avoid gathering decision-relevant evidence. Do not prescribe the answer, samples or a new search direction; use the team's stated comparison and evidence. An incumbent can still be preferred on measured tradeoffs, but that rationale must be explicit.

A changed retained Er/Zr branch requires its own matched Ni support under R3; necessary follow-up qualification is part of that refinement, not unrelated search expansion. Make each delegation answer one scientific question or execute its recorded calculation. Request concise relevant contributions; do not reopen accepted reports without a new error or a changed composition.

For a new coarsening screen, lower J is an improvement; J < the incumbent best J + the task tolerance remains a contender pending feasibility. Do not interpret a tie-only handoff as permission to discard a better result. If an early screen rejects a challenger, retain the incumbent provisionally and check which required comparisons remain unmeasured before planning closure.

For a stored-nomination rationale correction, reuse a capable team with planner in execute mode and execute_intent=nominate; call record_recommendation with the same IDs and corrected full rationale, then return to scientific review. Existing verification remains valid only for unchanged preferred ID and numerical evidence. This operation is available after verification; it is not execute/calculate. A changed alloy needs qualification and its own verification.
