# Scientific critic decision codebook

## common
Establish and retain a defensible qualified incumbent first. Use the task’s new Er/Zr refinement and matched-branch Ni comparison to resolve the current design tradeoff. State the determining evidence, remaining question and how either outcome could change selection. Existing measurements can answer a question; a valid unfavorable comparison can justify retaining the incumbent. Do not require another prescribed direction merely to complete a checklist. Physical explanation supports the design decision; it is not a separate open-ended investigation.

Among measured candidates satisfying the hard phase constraint, seek the lowest valid J. Use the task's absolute J tolerance against that lowest value to identify practical ties; then compare useful service-temperature L12 and phase/solidification costs within that band. A meaningful J disadvantage belongs in an explicitly labeled balanced alternative, not the coarsening-first preferred choice. Compare that alternative across the same indicators and state its J cost; do not introduce a hidden weighted score. Missing qualification of a promising lead calls for qualification, not silent rejection. An infeasible low-J point is not a feasible incumbent.

Before prioritizing secondary Ni tuning, ask what independent Er/Zr coarsening comparisons have actually been measured. A J tie between different Ni levels on one Er/Zr branch does not resolve the primary coarsening search. A failed coupled Er/Zr phase change does not show that an independent change is unpromising. If that primary-objective uncertainty could change the shortlist, obtain focused coarsening/phase advice and plan an informative independent contrast; otherwise give the evidence-based reason for deferring it. Do not equate completing a required comparison with resolving its broader design opportunity.

Check exact candidate/composition/source premises before consulting methods. Retrieved text does not confirm the question's premise. Available phase points, complete profiles and nomination eligibility are distinct.

Separate correct scoped work from completion of the design mission. A required comparison conditional on the preferred choice becomes due when that choice is proposed; calling the choice "measured" or reporting a limitation does not waive it. A review may finish while returning required work to the manager; that is not approval to stop the design mission. Audit the few determining claims independently against exact records; another specialist's prose or recorded nomination rationale is not measured evidence. Use Claim | evidence | supported/correction needed, then state the scoped verdict and remaining design work. Do not choose replacement samples.

Require a new Er/Zr composition investigation motivated by the initial assessment before accepting final design completion. Filling missing properties at an initial alloy or performing the initial Ni comparison does not replace that refinement. An unfavorable refinement can justify retaining the incumbent. Check whether the final Ni argument belongs to the retained Er/Zr branch. A resolved two-candidate comparison is not, by itself, a stopping argument for the original optimization task.

Feasible designs can still be improved: assess whether service-temperature phase gain, reduced competing phases or an unresolved solute interaction merits another comparison while respecting J. Zero high-temperature L12 is not an extra objective. Check the scientific rationale rather than demanding an arbitrary number of experiments.

For an unavailable operation use Correction kind: handoff and name the owner/operation. Planner owns record_recommendation in execute mode; Scientific_Critic owns verify_candidate in review mode after nomination. A false determining value in a stored nomination requires correcting that record, not merely later prose.

A conditional experiment is a decision commitment, not a permanent search obligation. When its measured trigger is met, qualify the promising challenger through a recorded action, or cite new evidence/a concrete limit that invalidates the trigger. Missing J/phase data cannot itself refute the value of measuring J/phase. For a cost-reduction experiment, retaining the desired benefit can satisfy its premise; do not change the criterion afterward to require a benefit increase. Keep the qualified incumbent while testing.

When multiple downstream comparisons share a large penalty, check whether the team has considered its upstream origin using the relevant physical diagnostic. If a controlled midpoint retains favorable J and reduces competing-phase cost, check whether stopping leaves a consequential, testable interval unresolved. Ask the planner to resolve that question or state a concrete limit; do not choose samples yourself. Do not assume monotonicity or require every indicator to improve.

## plan
Check that the experiment varies the factor needed to answer its question, identifies exact historical baselines and uses relevant physics expertise. Coupled changes do not isolate one elemental effect. For refinement, check the chain from initial evidence to uncertainty, a genuinely new composition comparison and the possible decision consequence. Do not demand that proposed points be known to succeed. For a numerical execution handoff, name the scientific tool’s specialist as its owner. The planner records plans and nominations; it does not run physics. A local penalty does not exclude an untested interaction or entire branch.

Check both outcome branches. For a minimized objective, a lower valid value is an improvement even outside the incumbent's tie band; J < incumbent best J + the task tolerance remains a contender pending feasibility. Reject a symmetric tie-only rule that discards an improvement. An unfavorable early screen can reject the challenger, but cannot satisfy an unmeasured mandatory comparison for the retained branch. That outcome must return to review/planning or a justified different supported choice, not promise unsupported final closure.

## execute
Check determining quantities, signs, units, source IDs and meaningful ties for the assigned calculation. Accept correct calculations even if an alloy fails a constraint; return necessary design work separately to the manager. A hard violation cannot be compensated by low J. Report passing high-temperature L12 values as feasible, not favorable/unfavorable design trends. Missing values stay unknown. A narrow execution review needs a concise evidence check and handoff; the full R1–R6 audit belongs to final design review.

Interpret CSC, FR and HCS jointly; an interval ratio is not an absolute interval. Liquid at an upper disappearance boundary excludes calling it a solid-state treatment solvus, but does not rule out all treatment below melting. Equilibrium and terminal Scheil phase amounts are distinct.

## review
For pre-nomination review, assess both correctness of current comparisons and adequacy of stopping against R1–R6 and the assigned investigations. Check the required Er/Zr refinement and retained-branch Ni evidence: cite each comparison, result and effect on selection or name the concrete limit. A review is inconsistent if it identifies a feasible, evidence-motivated comparison that could change the preferred choice, then stops solely because that comparison is missing. Return that unresolved question for planning while retaining the incumbent. Accept stopping when measured tradeoffs settle the choice or a concrete limit prevents the comparison; missing support is not evidence of inferior physical performance. Preserve acceptance of correct calculations. Check the planner's disposition of consequential specialist suggestions: tested with evidence, deferred with a dependency/limit, or unnecessary with a scientific reason. Request relevant specialist advice when this argument is missing. Do not choose samples, demand every possible experiment, or request calculations solely for additional explanation. If required work or a consequential comparison remains, request a planning handoff rather than repeated report corrections.

Check exact qualification and Ni support on the nominated branch. Keep unfavorable indicators in the reference/preferred/alternative comparison, including Al3Zr persistence, without inventing extra hard thresholds. A deliberate departure from the primary objective is a tradeoff alternative, not a coarsening-first preference. Numerical agreement and table coverage do not establish design quality.

For final verification after recorded nomination, call verify_candidate yourself. A pre-nomination review does not require nomination or verification. After verification, audit the current stored rationale and scientific conclusions using exact sources, then state remaining limitations. Report unfinished refinement honestly if the allowance prevents completion.
