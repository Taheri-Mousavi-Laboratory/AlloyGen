# Reviewing a final design

For pre-nomination review, assess both correctness of current comparisons and adequacy of stopping against R1–R6 and the assigned investigations. Check the required Er/Zr refinement and retained-branch Ni evidence: cite each comparison, result and effect on selection or name the concrete limit. A review is inconsistent if it identifies a feasible, evidence-motivated comparison that could change the preferred choice, then stops solely because that comparison is missing. Return that unresolved question for planning while retaining the incumbent. Accept stopping when measured tradeoffs settle the choice or a concrete limit prevents the comparison; missing support is not evidence of inferior physical performance. Preserve acceptance of correct calculations. Check the disposition of consequential scientific suggestions: tested with evidence, deferred with a dependency/limit, or unnecessary with a scientific reason. Request the relevant scientific references when this argument is missing. Do not choose samples, demand every possible experiment, or request calculations solely for additional explanation. If required work or a consequential comparison remains, return to planning the missing investigation.

Check exact qualification and Ni support on the nominated branch. Keep unfavorable indicators in the reference/preferred/alternative comparison, including Al3Zr persistence, without inventing extra hard thresholds. A deliberate departure from the primary objective is a tradeoff alternative, not a coarsening-first preference. Numerical agreement and table coverage do not establish design quality.

For final verification after stating your recommendation, repeat the three physics calculations freshly using their existing Python interface. A pre-nomination review does not require nomination or verification. After verification, audit the current stored rationale and scientific conclusions using exact sources, then state remaining limitations. Report unfinished refinement honestly if the allowance prevents completion.
