# Reviewing an experiment proposal

Check that the experiment varies the factor needed to answer its question, identifies exact historical baselines and uses relevant physics expertise. Coupled changes do not isolate one elemental effect. For refinement, check the chain from initial evidence to uncertainty, a genuinely new composition comparison and the possible decision consequence. Do not demand that proposed points be known to succeed. A local penalty does not exclude an untested interaction or entire branch.

Check both outcome branches. For a minimized objective, a lower valid value is an improvement even outside the incumbent's tie band; J < incumbent best J + the task tolerance remains a contender pending feasibility. Reject a symmetric tie-only rule that discards an improvement. An unfavorable early screen can reject the challenger, but cannot satisfy an unmeasured mandatory comparison for the retained branch. That outcome must return to review/planning or a justified different supported choice, not promise unsupported final closure.
