G1 in-silico Al-Ni-Er-Zr design investigation

Implemented conditions and checks
- Candidate compositions were supplied directly in at.% for Ni, Er and Zr; Al is the balance and candidate Y=Yb=0.
- Coarsening was evaluated only via the fixed normalized J metric at 250 C; lower J is better. The fixed benchmark has J=1.
- Main-shortlist phase feasibility was enforced as equilibrium L12 at 660 C <= 1.05 mol%.
- Equilibrium L12 and Al3Zr amounts are normalized mol% of the whole system; Scheil terminal phase amounts are normalized mol% of the original system.
- FR_K, CSC and HCS were compared separately with the supplied reference. They are screening proxies, not printability predictions.

No feasible candidate with valid J and qualifying 660 C L12 was found within the qualified set.

Evidence -> unresolved question -> informative comparison -> decision logic
- Initial screening asked whether lower J sits on an Er-rich, Zr-lean, or more balanced branch once the 660 C L12 feasibility limit is imposed.
- Consequential new Er/Zr refinement: Ni=2.0 with (Er,Zr)=(0.7,0.5) and (0.9,0.5) was added after the initial assessment to test whether a local Er increase could raise service-temperature L12 without unacceptable J or high-temperature-phase cost.
- Matched-branch Ni comparison was then performed at the retained Er/Zr branch Er=0.7, Zr=0.5 for Ni=1.5, 2.0, 2.5 and 3.0 to determine how much terminal ternary reservoir is gained relative to primary/competing terminal phases and FR/CSC/HCS costs.

No preferred candidate could be nominated from the current qualified evidence.

Limits
- This is an in-silico screening investigation using the supplied CALPHAD-based tools and fixed screening proxies only.
- Terminal Scheil L12 or ternary reservoir does not directly predict aged precipitate fraction, strength or printability.
- Small nominal differences outside the J practical-tie rule are not proof of robustness.
- If decisive evidence remains unresolved in the saved tables, the next design step should target the unresolved interval identified there rather than broad untargeted sampling.
